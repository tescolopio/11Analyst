Prioritized by observed point cost. Built around your selected match mode.

How are priorities selected?

Each observed point has equal weight. There is no ranked-mode, recency, or Elo multiplier. Longer matches contribute more points. A pattern needs at least five classified losses across two matches to become a priority. Validated points from unfinished matches count toward loss analysis.

Loss counts are not guaranteed recoverable points. Opponent strength, missing measurements, and small samples remain part of the evidence. Practice targets are coaching suggestions, not measured requirements.

Confirmed conventions and input contract

Right-handed frame: +X is receiver right, +Y is up, +Z points from opponent toward receiver. The receiver faces -Z. Topspin is +X; backspin is -X. Overhead-clockwise sidespin is -Y; counterclockwise is +Y. Camera and playing handedness do not alter these physical vectors. No left/right sidespin naming is imposed.

Rev/s converts to rad/s with 2 pi. Controls use conservative visualization ranges, not skill thresholds. Mixed spin holds total magnitude constant. Missing angular velocity is unknown, not zero. This prototype accepts synthetic inputs only; raw log axes and units have not been converted to this frame.

Run a repeatable serve-feed check in Eleven
        
This requires your in-game observations. A successful key download is not a placement, spin or speed calibration.

        
  1. Back up your settings. Load an existing serve-return key and select just its representative serve feed (ANLY01 in the Analyst serve-return collection). Leave its settings unchanged during the test.
  2. Choose and describe one broad receiving-side target region before testing. The proposal below is a repeatability check, not a log-derived personal threshold.
  3. Test 20 feeds: the proposed gate is 20 legal serve feeds (both required bounces), at least 18 in the chosen target, and a comfortable pace. Count invalid feeds separately from your return errors.
  4. Record legal returns only out of valid feeds. Report spin as unknown unless you can identify it. No particular spin family or measured speed is required by this broad pilot.

        Provider API & verification limits
          
        Read public settings for key 4008.48 (external). This community GET endpoint separates the player ID (4008) from the settings ID (48). It is not an official upload API. Cloud settings viewer (external).
        
          
        The 11 Hero editor (external) uses GET /api/getBallMachineList, POST /api/sendBallMachineToCloud with a json settings object, and GET /api/getBallMachine. This is an observed, undocumented third-party interface; availability, permissions, and compatibility may change. We do not use game login tokens or bypass an authentication challenge.
        
          
        The Analyst compares new IDs against the pre-upload list and retrieves their settings. Exactly one complete match, including the publication tag, is required. It does not simply assume the highest ID belongs to you. Server normalization or listing delays may leave a real upload unverified; Recheck never posts it again.
        
          
        Read-back verification is an observation at the recorded time, not a guarantee the remote key will never change. The offline report stays network-disabled. The local server contacts 11 Hero only after explicit upload or recheck actions.

        Ball and contact measurements
        Useful signals. Clear gaps. No invented paddle angles or physical units.
        
      
        
      
        
        
        What the evidence can tell you
        • Scores confirm individual point losses.
        • Logged reasons identify broad loss outcomes.
        • Paired ball states describe observed incoming and outgoing contacts.
        • Paddle speed, face angle, and continuous swing paths remain unavailable.
        All ball vectors and rates remain in raw log units. Paired incoming/outgoing states are not full motion capture.
